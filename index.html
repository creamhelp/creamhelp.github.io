Shrink videos, keep the quality.

LeanVid compresses the videos in your photo library by lowering only the bitrate — resolution, frame rate, color, and HDR stay intact. Every result is verified before it replaces the original, and your originals stay recoverable for 30 days.

  • Quality-preserving compression — bitrate-only, HDR and every track preserved
  • Verified, safe replacement — integrity checks, recovery journal, system confirmation
  • See it yourself — side-by-side and slider comparison, full spec table
  • No waiting — background compression with Live Activity progress
  • On-device processing — your videos are compressed on your iPhone and are never uploaded; no account required
