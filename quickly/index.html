AI writes it for you

Describe the situation in one line, or paste a message you already send. You get it back with the changing parts as variables. Tap Shorter or More polite to fine-tune.

Pick your AI

Apple Intelligence on your device, for free; the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini app you already use; or your own API key. Or skip AI and write it yourself.

Right from your keyboard

In Messages, Mail or any chat app, pick a template and fill in the values. Type as usual, too, in 11 languages.

No calculator needed

Tomorrow or this Saturday in one tap, amounts with the currency symbol added for you, and formulas that fill in the total.

Reuse and find

Every message you fill in is kept in Finished Texts, ready to use again. Sort with tags and search by title or content.

Same on iPhone and iPad

Templates, tags and Finished Texts stay in sync on devices with the same iCloud account. Export a backup file anytime. Light and Dark Mode.

Only you can see your messages

Your messages are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud, where not even the developer can see them. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and the keyboard never goes online — Privacy
