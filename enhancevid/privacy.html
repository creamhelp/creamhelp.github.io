Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

Summary: Your videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. To keep EnhanceVid free, the app shows ads and uses analytics — details below.

Videos and photos

Frame-rate and resolution conversion and noise reduction all happen on your device. EnhanceVid never uploads your videos or photos to us or to third parties. Photo library access is used only to read the video you pick and to save the result as a new item. Originals are never changed or deleted.

Advertising

EnhanceVid shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process device information, the advertising identifier, approximate location (based on IP address) and ad interaction data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before personalized ads. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking through App Tracking Transparency; if you deny or don't consent, non-personalized ads are shown. Either way, every feature works the same.

Analytics and crash reports

EnhanceVid uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes so we can fix and improve it. This includes app events such as the number of videos enhanced or features used — never the content of your videos. Crash reports include diagnostic information about the device and the error.

No account

EnhanceVid requires no account or sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Enhancement history and quality scores are kept only in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you delete an entry from history or delete the app. Result videos saved to Photos or Files are yours and remain after the app is deleted.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, review or withdraw ad consent in the app, and remove all app data by deleting the app.

Third parties

Ads and analytics are provided by Google. Google's processing follows the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

EnhanceVid is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Ads shown in the app follow Google's policies.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the new version.
